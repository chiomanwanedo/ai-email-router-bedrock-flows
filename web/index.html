How we reply

One inbox, sorted the moment your message lands

Our office assistant reads each message and passes it to the right team with a reply drafted for them to check. Nothing goes out until a person has read it. Anything urgent or sensitive skips the queue and goes straight to our duty manager.

Your messageSorted in secondsThe right team replies

  • Repairs

    Leaks, broken fittings, appliances. Our maintenance team arranges a visit within 2 working days.

  • Viewings and lettings

    Booking a viewing, pets, applying, guarantors. Our lettings team replies within a working day.

  • Rent and deposit

    Payments, standing orders, deposits back at the end of a tenancy. Our accounts team checks and replies.

  • Needs a person now

    Gas, flooding, safety, complaints or legal matters. No automatic reply: the duty manager contacts you.

How do I report a repair?

Send us a message with what is wrong and your address. Our maintenance team will reply within 2 working days to arrange a visit, and may ask for a photo and the times someone can get in.

When will I get my deposit back?

Your deposit is held in a government-approved scheme. After the check-out visit we agree any deductions with you, then ask the scheme to release it. Our accounts team will confirm the dates for your tenancy.

Can I keep a pet?

Many of our landlords consider pets. Ask before you sign or before the pet arrives, and our lettings team will check with the landlord for you.

I paid my rent twice. What now?

Message us with the dates and amounts. Our accounts team checks what we received and arranges a refund of anything paid twice.

Who reads my message?

A person in our office, always. Our assistant only sorts messages and drafts a reply for the team to check. Urgent and sensitive messages go to the duty manager with no automatic reply.

Need something from the office?

Send one message. It reaches the right team, and a person replies.
